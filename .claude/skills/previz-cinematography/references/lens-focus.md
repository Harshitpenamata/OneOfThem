# Lenses and focus

Distilled from StudioBinder's guides on camera lenses and camera focus. These are paraphrased notes.

## Lens families

The categories are relative to the format. They're stated here for full frame; Super 35 is about 1.5× tighter, so a 35 mm on S35 frames roughly like a 50 mm on full frame.

- **Prime** – a fixed focal length. Better image, and it forces you to move the camera to a deliberate position instead of zooming. **This previz uses primes.**
- **Zoom** – a variable focal length. Needed only for a zoom move or a dolly zoom. Parfocal zooms hold focus through the zoom.
- **Wide angle** (below a standard lens) – more environment, exaggerated depth, faster-feeling movement, faces distort when close.
- **Standard** (about the sensor diagonal) – the most "natural" attention cone. On S35 that's roughly 28–35 mm.
- **Long focus / telephoto** – compresses depth, isolates the subject, separates it from the background, voyeuristic distance.
- **Fisheye** – extreme wide with strong distortion; strictly stylistic.
- **Macro** – near life-size reproduction of tiny subjects; for inserts.

How each length reads for this project (S35):

| mm | Feel | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| 18 | Very wide, expansive, distorts near objects | Car interior masters, tight spaces |
| 24 | Wide, immersive, deep | Establishing shots, wides, the crash-site master, the wide two-shot S04-09 |
| 32–35 | Natural, slightly wide | Medium shots, walking, OTS mediums, full shots |
| 50 | Neutral to intimate | MCUs, cleaner dialogue singles |
| 75–85 | Portrait compression, isolation | CUs, the close two-shot "lips ahead" beat |
| 100 macro | Detail, flattened | Phone, clock, puddle, radio inserts (step back for clearance) |

## Focus and depth of field

- **Deep focus** – everything sharp from the foreground to the background. Multi-plane staging, fewer cuts, the audience chooses where to look. Small aperture, wide lens.
- **Shallow focus** – one plane sharp. Isolates a subject without going closer and steers the eye hard. Wide aperture, long lens, close distance.
- **Soft focus** – the whole frame glows or blurs. Dream, memory, unreality. Diffusion filter or post.
- **Rack focus** – shift the plane between subjects during the shot. Redirects attention without a cut.
- **Split diopter** – two separate focal planes at once. Looks unnatural, so use it deliberately.
- **Tilt-shift** – a tilted plane of focus; the miniature look. Rare and stylised.

Previz default: **f/16 with manual focus on the subject** so blocking reads clearly. Use deliberate shallow focus (f/2–2.8) only where the story wants isolation. Key `ManualFocusDistance` to the subject distance, and key a rack where the shot calls for one.
